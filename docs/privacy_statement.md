## Privacy Statement

**Effective date:** Feb 7, 2026

Hyppo is designed to help you capture and organize research. This privacy statement explains what data we handle, how it is used, and the choices you have.

### Summary
- Hyppo stores your research content on your device by default.
- Network features (if enabled) may send data to third-party services to deliver those features.
- Optional analytics are opt-in and can be disabled at any time.

### Data We Handle
**Data you provide**
- Research content (questions, drivers, evidence, notes, tags).
- Files or links you attach to entries.

**Data generated by the app**
- App configuration and preferences.
- Timestamps, reminders, and workflow metadata.

**Optional diagnostics and analytics**
- Basic usage metrics and crash diagnostics, only if you opt in.

### How We Use Data
- Provide core functionality such as creating, editing, and organizing research.
- Improve stability and performance when you enable diagnostics or analytics.
- Deliver optional features that rely on network services.

### Network Features and Third-Party Services
Some features may require sending data to external services (for example, AI summaries or cloud sync). When you enable these features, the relevant data is transmitted to those providers only to perform the requested action. Details are shown in the feature UI where applicable.

### Sharing
Hyppo does not sell your personal data. We only share data when:
- You enable a feature that requires a third-party service.
- We are required to do so by law.

### Data Storage and Retention
- Your content remains on your device unless you enable a sync or external feature.
- Optional diagnostics and analytics are retained only as long as needed for their stated purpose.

### Security
We take reasonable measures to protect your data. No system is 100% secure, and you should avoid storing sensitive information you are not comfortable keeping on your device.

### Your Choices
- Disable optional analytics or diagnostics in app settings.
- Avoid using features that require external services if you prefer to keep data local.

### Children's Privacy
Hyppo is not intended for use by children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

### Changes to This Statement
We may update this statement as the product evolves. We will update the effective date at the top of this document when changes are made.

### Contact
If you have questions about privacy, contact us at: `privacy@hyppo.app`.
